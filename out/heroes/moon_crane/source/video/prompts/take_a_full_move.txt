The same stocky heroic cartoon spear-warrior about 3 heads tall with a big head, huge fists and short legs, slate-blue armour with a large gold crane emblem on the chest, a giant cream crane-wing shoulder guard on his left shoulder, black hair in a high topknot with a brick-red cord, glowing pale-gold eyes, brown boots with gold trim, and an oversized spear with a bright silver leaf-shaped spearhead, a gold crescent cross-guard and a brick-red tassel, in one continuous spear spin-sweep.
START (0 to 0.6 s): he stands in the calm idle stance of the still, then his free hand comes up so that BOTH huge fists grip the haft, the spear held level at chest height.
1. WIND-UP (0.6 to 1.5 s): he twists his hips, shoulders and head away from the camera until his BACK faces the camera, the weight on the far leg and the near foot on its toe; both fists pull the haft behind his body so that the haft lies level at chest height pointing to the LEFT of the frame, only the spearhead sticking out past his back on the left; the coat tails hang behind him; he looks to the right over his shoulder.
2. SWEEP (1.5 to 3.3 s): BOTH hands still grip the haft; the hips drive first, then the shoulders, then the arms; the spear swings in ONE big horizontal circle seen slightly from above, the spearhead travelling from behind on the left, across the far side of his body, to the right; in the middle of the swing his chest has opened toward the camera and the level haft points to the RIGHT of the frame in front of his chest, both fists gripping, the spearhead flat and leading, the coat tails and the tassel flung backward.
3. FOLLOW-THROUGH (3.3 to 4.3 s): the swing keeps going across the near side in front of his chest; his left hand lets go; only his RIGHT hand (the hand on the left of the frame) holds the haft; the spear is flung by momentum around behind his back and ends level behind his waist with the spearhead sticking out to the LEFT of the frame; his body is now facing the camera, the front knee bent, the weight forward, his free arm swung out for balance.
END (4.3 to 5 s): he HOLDS that pose completely still.
The order is always: grip with both hands, wind-up with the back to the camera, horizontal sweep to the right, follow-through, one-handed finish with the spear behind his back, hold.
