Game enemy lineup sheet: TEN different melee enemy sprites of one ice clan arranged in a strict grid of 5 columns by 2 rows on ONE image, every character in its own invisible cell of equal size, each cell one fifth of the image width and one half of the image height. The top row holds the light, fast units and the bottom row the casters and the heavy units. Each character is drawn with the body shape of its role, so the role reads from the silhouette alone: the archers and the spear thrower are tall, thin and long-limbed, the assassin is lean and leans forward with sharp angles, the gladiator is a wide square block, the warlord is tall and commanding. Each is a chunky grim fantasy cartoon character, never cute, never a mascot. All TEN are in SIDE VIEW from a high top-down camera, facing RIGHT toward the right edge of the image, the body turned only about 30 degrees toward the viewer, the far arm and leg partly hidden behind the body, standing UPRIGHT in a calm ready stance with both feet on ONE horizontal ground line, the head tilted about 8 degrees forward. Every biped character has TWO ARMS OF EXACTLY THE SAME SIZE AND LENGTH and TWO LEGS OF EXACTLY THE SAME SIZE AND LENGTH; the Frost Wolf stands on FOUR LEGS of exactly the same size. Every character has a face built from the fewest bold shapes, TWO BOLD GLOWING eye shapes with no pupils, skin in two flat tones. Layout rules: each character is VERY SMALL inside its cell and centered horizontally in it, with the soles of the feet on a line at 70% of the cell height, a very wide empty background space on both sides of every character and an empty band above the head; the ten characters are far apart, clearly separated by empty background, and no weapon, hand or cloak ever reaches the neighbouring cell. Sizes: characters 1 to 8 are all the same medium size, even the small ones, with the same amount of detail; characters 9 and 10 are larger, about one and a half times that height; the Glacier Gladiator is the widest and the Frost Warlord the tallest of all ten.
